MYMINI164 is run by one person. There is no support desk and no ticket system, but every message is read.

Email: help@mybatsurvey.co.uk

What to write about

  • Something in the catalogue is wrong or missing. If you have an account, the quickest route is inside the application: "Can't find your car?" and the report button on any model both go straight into the queue with the release attached.
  • Something is broken. Say what you did, what happened, and what you expected. Which browser and whether it was a phone or a computer helps more than it sounds like it should.
  • Your data. A copy of it, a correction to it, or its deletion. See the privacy notice; the first and the last you can do yourself from Your Account.
  • A security problem. Please write before publishing it anywhere, and it will be looked at straight away.

What not to expect

Valuations, authentication, or an opinion on whether to buy something. The figures in the application are estimates and are labelled as such.
